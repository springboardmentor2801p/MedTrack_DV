# MedTrack_DV – Hospital Operations & Patient Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

**MedTrack_DV** is a Hospital Operations and Patient Analytics Dashboard developed using **Power BI** to monitor hospital performance, patient flow, departmental efficiency, and resource utilization.

The project transforms hospital operational data into meaningful **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)** and interactive visualizations that support data-driven healthcare management and operational decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Monitor overall hospital performance.
* Analyze patient admissions and discharges.
* Track patient flow and average length of stay.
* Compare performance across hospital departments.
* Monitor bed occupancy and utilization.
* Analyze departmental revenue and efficiency.
* Monitor staff and equipment allocation.
* Support healthcare capacity planning and operational decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization
* **Python** – Data processing and KPI generation
* **CSV / Excel** – Dataset and analytical outputs
* **DAX** – KPI calculations and measures
* **Data Visualization** – Charts, cards, gauges and analytical visuals

# 📑 Dashboard Modules

## 1. 🏥 Hospital Overview

The **Hospital Overview** page provides a high-level view of hospital performance.

### Features

* Total Admissions
* Average Length of Stay
* Readmission Rate
* Occupancy Rate
* Bed Utilization
* Department Efficiency
* Monthly admission trends
* Admissions by department
* Occupancy Rate by Department
* Readmission analysis

The overview dashboard uses admission date, discharge date and department filters to allow users to analyze hospital performance interactively.

---

## 2. 👥 Patient Flow

The **Patient Flow** page focuses on patient movement throughout the hospital.

### Features

* Admission Trends by Month
* Discharge Trends by Month
* Total Admissions
* Total Discharges
* Patient Movement by Year and Department
* Average Length of Stay by Department
* Patient Count by Month

## The dashboard enables users to identify admission and discharge patterns and understand patient movement across different departments and years.

## 3. 🏢 Department Analytics

The **Department Analytics** page compares operational performance between hospital departments.

### Features

* Total Departments
* Average Length of Stay
* Department Efficiency
* Top Performing Department
* Total Revenue
* Patient Volume by Department
* Department Efficiency Comparison
* Revenue by Department
* Treatment Capacity Analysis

The dashboard identifies **Endocrinology** as the top-performing department and displays total revenue of approximately **₹2.79M**.

---

## 4. 🏨 Resource Utilization

The **Resource Utilization** page focuses on efficient use of hospital resources.

### Features

* Total Admissions
* Occupancy Rate
* Bed Utilization
* Occupied Bed Days
* Bed Utilization by Department
* Revenue and Occupied Bed Days
* Staff Allocation Monitoring
* Capacity Planning
* Resource Availability Analysis
* Equipment Utilization Tracking

## Equipment utilization includes resources such as **Ventilators, Defibrillators, ECG Machines, MRI Scanners, and Dialysis Machines**.
